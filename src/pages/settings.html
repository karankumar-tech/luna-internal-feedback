ReportsSettings

Settings

Report categories, beta users, the AI diagnosis queue and test data.

Report categories

Rename a category (edit, then press Enter), deactivate it, or add a new one. A deactivated category leaves the app's form but keeps its history.

Beta users BETA

Production users who get the feedback option in the Luna app. Their reports arrive tagged BETA, and Source → Beta users on Reports lists them. A name shows beside the ID on their reports, so the team knows who is who; it never leaves the dashboard. The app checks once a day, so an added or removed user sees the change within a day.

AI diagnosis

Runs on demand from a report's page. Requests made before the evening log sync wait and finish automatically.

Test data

Reports sent with is_test: true (demo rows and app integration runs) sit alongside real ones with a TEST tag. Delete them whenever you want; real reports are never touched.
