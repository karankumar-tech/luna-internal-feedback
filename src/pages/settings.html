ReportsSettings

Settings

Report categories, the AI diagnosis queue and test data.

Report categories

Rename a category (edit, then press Enter), deactivate it, or add a new one. A deactivated category leaves the app's form but keeps its history.

AI diagnosis

Runs on demand from a report's page. Requests made before the evening log sync wait and finish automatically.

Test data

Reports sent with is_test: true (demo rows and app integration runs) sit alongside real ones with a TEST tag. Delete them whenever you want; real reports are never touched.
