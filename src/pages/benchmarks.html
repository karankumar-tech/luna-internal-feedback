Import from an Apple Health export

Wear Luna and another device for the same workout or night, let both sync to Apple Health, then export. The file is read here in your browser and is not uploaded or kept: only the sessions you pick are saved.

Drop export.zip here

or the export.xml inside it. A few hundred megabytes is fine.

1 · On the iPhoneHealth app, your profile picture (top right), Export All Health Data. It makes export.zip.
2 · Get it hereAirDrop or save it to Files, then drop it on this page. Zipped or unzipped both work.
3 · Pick the sessionsYou will see every workout and night in the file, with the ones already imported marked.
